Ocean Wallet keeps you informed about activity on your account through timely notifications. You can customize which events trigger a notification and through which channels you receive them — giving you full visibility without unnecessary noise.

## Notification Channels

Ocean Wallet delivers notifications through two channels:

- **Email** — Sent to your registered email address. Available to all users on any platform.
- **Push notifications** — Delivered to your mobile device via the Ocean Wallet app. Available when you have the app installed and have granted notification permissions.

You can enable or disable each notification type independently for each channel.

## Available Notifications

The table below lists every notification type, what triggers it, and its default state.

| Notification | Trigger | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Incoming payment** | Someone sends money to your wallet | On |
| **Payment sent** | Your outgoing send is confirmed | On |
| **Deposit received** | A bank deposit settles to your wallet | On |
| **Withdrawal completed** | Your withdrawal reaches your bank account | On |
| **Payment link paid** | Someone completes a payment via your payment link | On |
| **Security alerts** | New device login, password change, or 2FA change | On — cannot be disabled |
| **Marketing & product updates** | News, tips, and feature announcements | Off |

## Changing Notification Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap or click **Settings** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    Select **Notifications** from the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Each Notification">
    Use the toggle next to each notification type to turn it on or off. You can adjust settings independently for **Email** and **Push** channels where the option appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Preferences">
    Click **Save** to apply your changes. Your new preferences take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Security alerts cannot be disabled.** Notifications for new device logins, password changes, and 2FA changes are always sent to your registered email address. This protects you in case someone else gains access to your account.
</Note>
