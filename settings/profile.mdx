Your Ocean Wallet profile controls how you appear to other users and how the platform contacts you. Keeping your information current ensures you receive important notifications, that senders can find you easily, and that your account stays secure.

## Updating Your Name

Your display name appears in transaction receipts and search results when other users look you up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap or click **Settings** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Profile">
    Select **Profile** from the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Name">
    Click **Edit Name**, then type your updated first and last name in the fields provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save** to apply your new name. The change takes effect immediately across your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing Your Email

Your email address is your primary login credential and the address Ocean Wallet uses for all account communications.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Profile">
    Select **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change Email">
    Click **Change Email** and enter your new email address in the field provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your New Address">
    Check your new inbox for a confirmation link from Ocean Wallet. Click the link to verify ownership and complete the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing your email also updates the address associated with your wallet — anyone searching for your wallet by email will need to use your new address going forward.
</Note>

## Profile Photo

Your profile photo helps senders confirm they're paying the right person before completing a transfer.

To upload or update your photo, go to **Settings → Profile → Photo**, then select an image file from your device. Your photo is visible to other Ocean Wallet users when they search for your wallet by name or email.

For the best results, use a square image of at least **200 × 200 px** in **JPG or PNG** format. Large files are automatically resized on upload.

## Changing Your Password

Regularly updating your password is one of the most effective ways to protect your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Security">
    Select **Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change Password">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Current Password">
    Type your existing password to confirm your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter and Confirm Your New Password">
    Type your new password, then retype it in the confirmation field to ensure they match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You'll remain logged in on your current device, but other active sessions will be signed out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a strong, unique password that you don't reuse on any other site. A password manager makes this easy to maintain.
</Tip>

## Closing Your Account

If you wish to close your Ocean Wallet account, contact our support team at **support@oceanwallet.com** to begin the process.

Before your account can be closed:

- Your wallet balance must be **$0.00**. Withdraw or transfer any remaining funds before submitting your request.
- Any pending transactions must be fully settled.

<Warning>
  Per regulatory requirements, your account data is retained for **90 days** after closure before it is permanently deleted. You will not be able to access your account during this period. Make sure to download any transaction history you need before requesting closure.
</Warning>
