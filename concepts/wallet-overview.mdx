Ocean Wallet is a digital wallet that lets you hold a balance, send and receive money, and manage payments entirely from one account. Whether you're paying a friend, collecting funds through a payment link, or moving money to your bank, everything flows through your wallet — accessible from any device, at any time.

## Your Wallet

Each Ocean Wallet account comes with a single primary wallet that serves as the hub for all your financial activity. Your wallet holds a balance in USD, tracks every transaction you make, and connects to the funding sources you use to move money in and out.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One Primary Wallet" icon="wallet">
    Your account maps to exactly one primary wallet. All balances, transactions, and payment activity live here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessible Anywhere" icon="globe">
    Manage your wallet from the web dashboard, a mobile device, or directly through the Ocean Wallet API.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Ocean Wallet balances are held in USD. International currencies are not currently supported.
</Note>

## Wallet ID

Your wallet ID is a unique identifier assigned to your account when you sign up. It looks like this:

```
ocn_wlt_abc123xyz
```

Share your wallet ID with anyone who needs to send you money. Alternatively, you can share the email address registered to your Ocean Wallet account — both route payments directly to your wallet.

<Tip>
  Find your wallet ID at any time by navigating to **Account Settings → Wallet Details** in the dashboard.
</Tip>

## Funding Sources

Funding sources are the external bank accounts or debit and credit cards you connect to Ocean Wallet. They act as the on-ramp and off-ramp for your wallet balance.

- **Deposits** — Pull funds from a connected bank account or card into your wallet balance.
- **Withdrawals** — Push funds from your wallet balance back out to a connected bank account.

<Info>
  Funds must clear from your funding source before they appear in your available balance. See [Balances & Transactions](/concepts/balances-and-transactions) for settlement timelines.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Funding Sources">
    In the dashboard, go to **Settings → Funding Sources**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Source">
    Select **Add Bank Account** or **Add Card**, then follow the verification prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Default">
    Mark one source as your default to speed up future deposits and withdrawals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contacts

Contacts let you save frequent recipients so you don't have to look up a wallet ID or email address every time you send money. A contact stores a display name alongside their wallet ID or registered email.

<Accordion title="How to add a contact">
  1. Navigate to **Contacts** in the dashboard sidebar.
  2. Select **New Contact**.
  3. Enter the recipient's name and their wallet ID or Ocean Wallet email address.
  4. Save the contact — they'll appear in your recipient list the next time you initiate a send.
</Accordion>
