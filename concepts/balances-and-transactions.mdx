Every action that moves money in or out of your Ocean Wallet — whether you're depositing from a bank, sending to a friend, or receiving a payment — creates a transaction record and affects your balance. Understanding how those pieces fit together helps you track your funds accurately and anticipate when money will be available.

## Your Balance

Your **available balance** is the amount you can send or withdraw right now. It reflects only funds that have fully settled in your wallet.

Deposits and incoming transfers that are still processing appear as a **pending balance** — separate from your available balance — until they settle. You cannot spend pending funds.

<Info>
  Your available balance and pending balance are both visible on the **Home** screen of the dashboard and in the `GET /wallets/{id}/balance` API endpoint.
</Info>

## Transaction Types

Ocean Wallet records every money movement as one of the following transaction types:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Send** | You initiate a payment from your wallet to another Ocean Wallet user. |
| **Receive** | Another user sends money to your wallet ID or registered email. |
| **Deposit** | Funds are added to your wallet balance from a connected bank account or card. |
| **Withdrawal** | Funds are moved from your wallet balance to a connected bank account. |
| **Payment link** | A payment collected by a recipient through a shareable link you created. |

## Transaction States

Each transaction moves through one or more states as it is processed. You can see the current state on every transaction detail page.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="pending" icon="clock">
    The transaction is being processed and has not yet settled. Funds are temporarily held and not available to either party.
  </Card>
  <Card title="completed" icon="circle-check">
    The transaction settled successfully. Available balances have been updated for the sender and recipient.
  </Card>
  <Card title="failed" icon="circle-xmark">
    The transaction could not be processed — for example, due to insufficient funds or a declined card. No money moved.
  </Card>
  <Card title="refunded" icon="rotate-left">
    The transaction was reversed and the original amount returned to the sender's wallet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  A `pending` transaction does not guarantee completion. Always confirm a `completed` state before treating funds as received.
</Warning>

## Settlement Times

How quickly a transaction moves from `pending` to `completed` depends on the transaction type:

<Accordion title="Deposits (bank account or card)">
  Deposits typically take **1–3 business days** to settle. The exact time depends on your bank and the payment method you use. Card deposits may settle faster than bank transfers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sends between Ocean Wallet users">
  Sends between two Ocean Wallet accounts are **instant**. The recipient's available balance updates as soon as you confirm the payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Withdrawals to a bank account">
  Withdrawals take **1–2 business days** to arrive in your connected bank account. Processing begins on the next business day after you initiate the withdrawal.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  View your full transaction history — including filters by type, state, and date range — in the **Transactions** tab of the dashboard, or retrieve it programmatically using the `/v1/transactions` API endpoint.
</Tip>
