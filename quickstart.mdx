This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a brand-new account to completing your first payment. By the end, you'll have a funded wallet and a clear picture of how Ocean Wallet works day-to-day.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following:

- A **free Ocean Wallet account** — you'll create one in Step 1 below.
- A **verified email address** that you have access to right now, since Ocean Wallet sends a confirmation link before you can log in.

## Set Up and Send Your First Payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.oceanwallet.com](https://app.oceanwallet.com) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your full name, email address, and a strong password, then click **Create Account**.

    Ocean Wallet will immediately send a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click **Verify My Email** to activate your account. You'll be redirected back to the app and logged in automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Identity Verification">
    To unlock sending funds and withdrawals, you need to verify your identity. From your dashboard, click **Verify Identity** and follow the prompts to upload a government-issued ID — a passport or driver's license both work.

    <Info>
      Basic features such as receiving funds and viewing your balance are available immediately without verification. Full sending and withdrawal capabilities are unlocked once your ID is approved.
    </Info>

    Most submissions are reviewed and approved within minutes. You'll receive an email confirmation as soon as the review is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund Your Wallet">
    With your account verified, you're ready to add money. Click **Add Funds** on your dashboard, then choose one of the available deposit methods:

    - **Bank transfer** — link your bank account and initiate an ACH transfer.
    - **Debit or credit card** — enter your card details for an instant deposit.

    The minimum deposit amount is **$10**. Your balance will update as soon as the funds clear — instantly for card deposits, and within one business day for bank transfers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Your First Payment">
    Click **Send** on your dashboard to open the payment screen. Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | What to enter |
    |---|---|
    | **Recipient** | The recipient's email address or Ocean Wallet ID |
    | **Amount** | The amount you want to send (in USD) |
    | **Note** *(optional)* | A short message to accompany the payment |

    Review the payment summary, then click **Confirm & Send**. The recipient is notified instantly, and the funds appear in their wallet right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
