Getting your Ocean Wallet account fully configured takes only a few minutes and unlocks everything the platform has to offer — from instant peer-to-peer payments to bank withdrawals and advanced security controls. Follow the sections below in order to go from registration to a fully verified, secured account.

## Create Your Account

Start by registering for a free Ocean Wallet account directly from your browser or mobile device.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Sign-Up Page">
    Go to [app.oceanwallet.com](https://app.oceanwallet.com) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Your Details">
    Enter the following information in the registration form:

    - **Full name** — your legal first and last name.
    - **Email address** — use an address you check regularly; Ocean Wallet sends important notifications here.
    - **Password** — choose a strong, unique password that you don't use on any other site.

    Click **Create Account** when you're ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email Address">
    Check your inbox for an email from Ocean Wallet with the subject line **Verify your email address**. Click the **Verify My Email** button inside that email.

    You'll be redirected to the app and logged in automatically. If the email doesn't arrive within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Identity Verification

Ocean Wallet uses a Know Your Customer (KYC) process to comply with financial regulations and protect every user on the platform. Completing verification unlocks higher transaction limits and full withdrawal access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Verification Flow">
    From your dashboard, click **Verify Identity**. You can also find this option under **Settings > Identity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a Government-Issued ID">
    Take a clear photo or scan of one of the following documents:

    - **Passport** (photo page)
    - **Driver's license** (front and back)

    Upload the image(s) using the on-screen prompts. Make sure the document is fully in frame, well-lit, and all text is legible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a Selfie Photo">
    Follow the on-screen instructions to take a selfie or upload a recent photo of yourself. Ocean Wallet uses this to confirm that the ID you submitted belongs to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Approval">
    Most identity submissions are reviewed and approved **within minutes**. You'll receive an email notification as soon as a decision is made. Once approved, your account is upgraded automatically with no further action needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Sending more than **$500 per day** and all bank withdrawals require a completed and approved identity verification. Attempting these actions before verification will prompt you to complete KYC first.
</Warning>

## Link a Bank Account

Linking a bank account lets you deposit funds via ACH transfer and withdraw your balance directly to your bank.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Funding Sources">
    Go to **Settings > Funding Sources** in the left-hand navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Bank Account">
    Click **Add Bank Account**. You'll be presented with two connection methods:

    - **Instant connection** — log in to your bank securely through Ocean Wallet's bank-linking portal. Your account details are verified in seconds and nothing is stored on Ocean Wallet's servers.
    - **Manual entry** — enter your bank's **routing number** and your **account number** directly. Ocean Wallet will make two small verification deposits within one to two business days; confirm those amounts to complete linking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as Default (Optional)">
    Once your bank account is linked and verified, you can click **Set as Default** to make it the primary source for deposits and the default destination for withdrawals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Security Recommendations

Your account security is your responsibility as much as ours. Take a few extra minutes now to protect your funds.

<Note>
  **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).** Go to [Settings > Two-Factor Auth](/settings/two-factor-auth) and link an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy. With 2FA enabled, anyone trying to log in to your account needs both your password and a one-time code from your device.

  - Use a **strong, unique password** for your Ocean Wallet account — never reuse a password from another service.
  - **Never share your login credentials** with anyone, including Ocean Wallet support staff, who will never ask for your password.
  - Review your **active sessions** regularly under Settings > Security and revoke any devices you don't recognize.
</Note>
