Ocean Wallet makes it simple to move money between users in seconds. Whether you're splitting a bill, paying a friend back, or collecting a payment, the send and receive flow is designed to be fast, transparent, and secure from any device.

## Sending Money

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard and click Send">
    Log in to your Ocean Wallet account. From the main dashboard, click the **Send** button in the top action bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the recipient's details">
    Type the recipient's registered email address or their wallet ID in the **To** field. Ocean Wallet will look up the account and display the recipient's name for you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type the amount you want to send in USD. The field accepts up to two decimal places.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an optional note">
    Use the **Memo** field to include a short note for the recipient — for example, "Rent for June" or "Dinner last Friday." This note is visible to both parties in the transaction history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the recipient name, amount, and memo on the review screen. When everything looks correct, click **Confirm Send**. The recipient is notified instantly via email and in-app notification, and the funds appear in their balance right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Daily send limits apply based on your verification status:**
  - **Unverified accounts** — up to $500 per day
  - **Verified accounts** — up to $10,000 per day

  To increase your limit, complete identity verification in **Settings > Verification**.
</Note>

## Receiving Money

You don't need to take any action to receive money from another Ocean Wallet user. Anyone who knows your registered email address or wallet ID can send funds directly to your account, and the balance updates automatically the moment the transfer completes.

Received funds appear immediately in your **Available Balance** on the dashboard, along with a notification confirming the amount and the sender.

<Tip>
  Find your wallet ID by navigating to **Settings > Account**. Your wallet ID is a unique identifier you can share with anyone who wants to send you money — even if they don't have your email address.
</Tip>

## Cancelling a Send

Sends between Ocean Wallet users are processed instantly and **cannot be reversed** once you click **Confirm Send**. Always double-check the recipient and amount on the review screen before confirming.

If you believe a send was made in error or as a result of fraud, contact Ocean Wallet Support within **24 hours** of the transaction. While we cannot guarantee a recovery, our team will work with both parties to attempt a resolution.
