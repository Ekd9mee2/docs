Ocean Wallet connects directly to your bank accounts and debit cards so you can fund your wallet or move money back to your bank whenever you need to. Deposits and withdrawals are managed from your dashboard, and all linked sources are encrypted and stored securely.

## Making a Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Funds in the dashboard">
    From the main dashboard, click **Add Funds**. This opens the deposit flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a funding source">
    Select an existing linked bank account or debit card from the list, or add a new source by clicking **Add New**. See [Managing Funding Sources](#managing-funding-sources) below for instructions on linking accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the deposit amount">
    Type the amount you want to add to your wallet. The minimum deposit is **$10**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deposit">
    Review the funding source and amount, then click **Confirm Deposit**. You'll receive a confirmation notification once the transaction is initiated.

    - **Bank transfer** — funds typically arrive within **1–3 business days**.
    - **Linked debit card** — funds arrive **instantly**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Daily deposit limits by funding source:**
  - **Bank transfer** — up to $5,000 per day
  - **Debit card** — up to $1,000 per day

  These limits reset at midnight UTC.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Deposits made with a debit or credit card may incur a **1.5% processing fee**, which is shown on the confirmation screen before you commit to the transaction. Bank transfer deposits are free.
</Warning>

## Withdrawing to a Bank Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Withdraw in the dashboard">
    From the main dashboard, click **Withdraw**. This opens the withdrawal flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a linked bank account">
    Choose the bank account you want to withdraw to. Only linked bank accounts are eligible for withdrawals — cards cannot be used as withdrawal destinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the withdrawal amount">
    Type the amount you'd like to withdraw. The minimum withdrawal is **$1**, and the amount cannot exceed your current Available Balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the withdrawal">
    Review the destination account and amount, then click **Confirm Withdrawal**. Funds typically arrive in your bank account within **1–2 business days**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Withdrawals require a **verified identity**. If you haven't completed verification yet, you'll be prompted to do so before proceeding. Visit [Account Setup](/account-setup) to complete the process.
</Note>

## Managing Funding Sources

You can link multiple bank accounts and debit cards to your Ocean Wallet account to keep your preferred funding options always ready.

To add or remove a funding source:

1. Go to **Settings > Funding Sources**.
2. Click **Add Source** to link a new bank account or card, or click the **Remove** icon next to an existing source to unlink it.
3. Follow the on-screen prompts to verify ownership of the new source.

Keep in mind that while you can deposit from both bank accounts and debit cards, **only bank accounts can be used as withdrawal destinations**.
