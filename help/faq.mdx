The questions below cover the topics our users ask about most often. If you don't find what you're looking for here, our support team is available at **support@oceanwallet.com** and typically responds within one business day.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Ocean Wallet free to use?">
    Creating an Ocean Wallet account and sending money to other Ocean Wallet users is free. Withdrawing funds to a bank account incurs a small flat fee displayed at the time of the transaction. Receiving money is always free. Any applicable fees are shown in full before you confirm a transaction — there are no hidden charges.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I verify my identity?">
    Identity verification (KYC) is required to unlock higher transaction limits and certain features. To verify your identity, go to **Settings → Verification** and follow the on-screen prompts. You'll need to provide:

    - A government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID card)
    - A short selfie or live photo to confirm the ID belongs to you

    Verification typically completes within a few minutes but may take up to 24 hours during high-volume periods. You'll receive an email confirmation once your verification is approved.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the sending and deposit limits?">
    Ocean Wallet applies daily limits based on your verification status:

    | Action | Unverified | Verified |
    |---|---|---|
    | Send per day | $500 | $10,000 |
    | Deposit per day | $500 | $10,000 |
    | Withdrawal per day | $500 | $10,000 |

    Limits reset at midnight UTC each day. To increase your limits, complete identity verification in **Settings → Verification**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do deposits take?">
    Deposit timing depends on the method you use:

    - **Bank transfer (ACH)** — 1–3 business days after your bank processes the debit.
    - **Ocean Wallet to Ocean Wallet** — Instant.

    You'll receive a notification as soon as your deposit settles. If your bank transfer hasn't arrived after 3 business days, check your bank statement to confirm the debit went through, then contact support if the funds are still missing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send money internationally?">
    At this time, Ocean Wallet supports transfers between Ocean Wallet accounts only. International bank withdrawals are not yet available. We're actively working on cross-border support — sign up for product updates in **Settings → Notifications** to be notified when this feature launches.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currencies does Ocean Wallet support?">
    Ocean Wallet currently supports **US Dollars (USD)** only. All balances, transactions, and statements are denominated in USD. Support for additional currencies is on our roadmap, and we'll announce availability through our product update notifications.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I send to the wrong person?">
    Wallet-to-wallet transfers are processed instantly and cannot be automatically reversed. If you send to the wrong recipient, contact **support@oceanwallet.com** immediately with your transaction ID. Our team will reach out to the recipient on your behalf to request a voluntary return of funds. We cannot guarantee recovery, so always double-check the recipient's name and email address before confirming a send.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get a refund?">
    Ocean Wallet does not issue automatic refunds for completed transactions. If you need a refund for a payment made to another Ocean Wallet user, contact the recipient directly and ask them to send the funds back to you. If you believe a charge was unauthorized or fraudulent, email **support@oceanwallet.com** with your account email and the transaction ID, and our team will investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my money insured?">
    Funds held in your Ocean Wallet are held in FDIC-insured bank accounts through our banking partners, giving you up to **$250,000 in FDIC coverage** per depositor. This means your money is protected in the event of a bank failure. Ocean Wallet itself is not a bank, but your funds are always held in insured accounts. For more details, review our [Terms of Service](#).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    You can reach the Ocean Wallet support team through the following channels:

    - **Email:** support@oceanwallet.com — we aim to respond within one business day.
    - **In-app chat:** Available in the Ocean Wallet mobile app under **Help → Chat with Us** during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 am–6 pm ET).

    When contacting support, include your **account email address** and any relevant **transaction IDs** to help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
